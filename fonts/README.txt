Fonts used on deanbrickland.com
===============================

Self-hosted so visitors' browsers make no third-party requests (no calls
to Google or any other font host).

  archivo-latin.woff2             Archivo (variable, weights 400-600), Latin subset
  ibm-plex-mono-400-latin.woff2   IBM Plex Mono Regular, Latin subset

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence text,
with each font's copyright notice, is kept next to the files:

  OFL-Archivo.txt     https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo
  OFL-IBM-Plex.txt    https://github.com/IBM/plex

The WOFF2 files are the unmodified Latin-subset builds served by Google
Fonts. The Latin subset covers accented letters used in Irish names. Keep
the licence files with the fonts if they are ever moved or re-downloaded.
